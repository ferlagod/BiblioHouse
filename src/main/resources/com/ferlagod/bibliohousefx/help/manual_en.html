1. Quick Introduction

What BiblioHouse does: It is a modern, ultra-fast, private, and open-source personal library manager built with Java 21 and JavaFX. You can catalog both printed and digital books, read EPUB files natively, manage loans with automated overdue alerts, track annual reading challenges, and sync your library to your private NextCloud/WebDAV server with total digital sovereignty.

Who it's for: Designed from the ground up for book enthusiasts, collectors, researchers, and families without requiring technical setup.

System Requirements: macOS (Apple Silicon or Intel), Windows 10/11, or modern Linux. Java 21+ installed. 500 MB of free storage space.

Supported Languages (100%): English (en), Spanish (es), Galician (gl), Catalan (ca), Basque (eu), and Portuguese (pt). Change language instantly under Tools > Language or in Settings.

2. Command Palette & Shortcuts v2.2

BiblioHouse 2.2 introduces a floating Universal Command Palette (Spotlight / Raycast style) accessible instantly from anywhere in the application by pressing Ctrl+K (Windows/Linux) or ⌘+K (macOS).

What can you do with the Command Palette?

  • Instant book search: Type any fragment of a book title, author, or genre and press Enter to jump straight to its detail view.
  • Instant quick actions:
    • ➕ Add New Book (Ctrl/⌘+N)
    • 📷 Scan Barcode with Webcam
    • ⚙️ Open Settings & Preferences (Ctrl/⌘+,)
    • 📊 View Library Statistics
    • 📄 Export to PDF Document
    • 🌐 Export Interactive Web Catalogue
    • 🔍 Search for Duplicate Books
  • Jump to tabs: Type "Loans", "Wishlist", "Sagas" or use the direct tab shortcuts Ctrl/⌘+1 to Ctrl/⌘+6.

3. Getting Started (Access & Welcome)

Upon launching BiblioHouse you are greeted with two startup modes:

  • Local Account Mode (Recommended): Protect your library with an encrypted username and password stored strictly on your machine. No credentials ever leave your computer.
  • Guest Mode (Without Registration): Direct access to your collection without entering credentials. Data is saved locally as usual, ideal for quick testing.

If starting for the first time, click "Create account", choose your username and password, and sign in.

  • Left Sidebar ("My Shelves"): Filter books by categories (Fiction, Non-fiction, Poetry...) or logical filters ("E-books", "Wishlist").
    Annual Reading Challenge Widget: Redesigned with modern visual ring indicators and real-time calculation of your progress towards your yearly reading goal.
  • Central Workspace (Tabs):
    1. My Books: Redesigned cover grid, customizable pagination, and compact table view.
    2. Add / Manage: Search OpenLibrary / Google Books / Inventaire, webcam barcode scanner, and manual entry.
    3. Active Loans: Loan administration with overdue notifications.
    4. History: Full log of returned loans and reading stats.
    5. Wishlist: Future books you want to purchase or read.
    6. Sagas & Series: Series management with missing volume detection.

5. Catalogue Features v2.2

My Books Tab (Redesigned Cover Cards & Pagination)

In version 2.2, book cards have been upgraded with crisp typography, soft shadows, and smooth hover micro-animations:

  • Reading Status Badges: Easily see if a book is 📋 Pending, 📖 Reading, or ✅ Finished.
  • Built-in Reading Progress Bar: When reading digital books or tracking page counts, cards display an integrated progress bar showing the exact percentage finished.
  • Virtualized Pagination: To ensure instant responsiveness with thousands of books, switch between 24, 48, 96 or All books per page using the pagination footer controls.
  • Accessible Keyboard Navigation: Move through book cards seamlessly with arrow keys (←, →, ↑, ↓). The active card displays a high-contrast focus ring. Press Enter to inspect the book or Space to open its context menu.

Context Menu (Right-Click on Any Book)

  • 📖 Read E-book: Launches the integrated reader (for EPUB digital books).
  • 🤝 Loan this book: Creates an active loan assigned to a member.
  • ✏️ Edit details: Modify metadata, covers, personal notes, genres, logical shelf, and physical rack/slot.
  • 🖼️ Change cover: Pick any local image as the new cover art.
  • 🏷️ Change status: Toggle quickly between Pending, Reading, or Finished.
  • 🖨️ Generate QR Label (PDF): Prints a physical label with a QR code linking to book details.
  • 🗑️ Delete book: Removes the book after user confirmation.

Non-Blocking Missing Cover Search v2.2

Under Tools > "Search for missing covers", BiblioHouse inspects your collection in the background without freezing the UI. In version 2.2, it checks local e-book files first (extracting embedded covers automatically) before querying online APIs.

6. Built-in E-book Reader & Local Covers v2.2

Native Digital Reader (EPUB)

BiblioHouse includes a native reader engine so you can read your e-books without external tools:

  • 64 KB Chunked Local Streaming: Using an embedded loopback HTTP server, large illustrated e-books load instantaneously without exhausting system memory.
  • Atomic Progress Saving: As you read, your position is stored atomically without rewriting the entire database. Your exact reading location syncs seamlessly across computers.

Offline Local Cover Extraction v2.2

You no longer need an active internet connection to get covers for your digital books! Powered by EbookMetadataService:

  • EPUB Files: Parses the internal package manifest (ZIP + OPF) and extracts the original embedded cover image in high resolution.
  • PDF Files: Locally renders page 1 using Apache PDFBox to generate a clean cover thumbnail offline.
  • Drag & Drop: Simply drop any .epub or .pdf onto "My Books" or the book editing dialog to automatically populate title, author, and cover art.

7. Reading Diary & Physical Locations

Double-click any book to open its detailed inspector view:

  • Reading Diary: Add reflections, quotes, and reading notes linked to specific page numbers. Notes are saved locally and synced via NextCloud.
  • Exact Physical Shelf Location: Keep track of physical storage (e.g. "Living Room - Oak Bookshelf - Shelf 3") to always find your physical copies instantly.
  • Printable QR Labels: Generate PDF tags with QR codes to affix to physical book spines or inner covers.

8. Smart Loans and Members

Manage who borrows your books with automated control:

  • Smart Alerts & Native Notifications v2.2: If a loan exceeds its return limit (default 30 days), BiblioHouse displays a warning on startup and sends a native desktop notification.
  • Member Cards (PDF): Register friends, family, and club members with contact details and print membership cards with scannable barcodes.
  • Comprehensive History: View past loans, exact return dates, and borrower stats.

11. NextCloud Sync on Startup v2.2

Keep your collection synchronized across multiple computers using your own NextCloud server:

Automatic Two-Way Pull on Startup v2.2

In version 2.2, BiblioHouse automatically checks your NextCloud server in the background whenever you open the app. If newer changes or covers exist on the server, they download silently without freezing the window, followed by a desktop notification.

Portable & Relative Storage Paths v2.2

The biblioteca.json database now saves cover and e-book paths relatively. This allows seamless cross-platform syncing between macOS, Windows, and Linux without broken file links.

Step-by-Step Setup:

  • Go to Tools > Settings > Synchronization tab.
  • Enter your server URL (e.g. https://cloud.example.com).
  • Enter your NextCloud username and an App Password generated in NextCloud Security settings (never enter your master password).
  • Click "Test connection" and save. Your password is stored with military-grade AES-256-GCM encryption.

12. Troubleshooting

Symptom / Problem Probable Cause Recommended Fix
Ctrl+K does not trigger the Command Palette On Apple Mac systems the shortcut is ⌘ Command + K. Press Cmd+K on macOS or click the search box in the main window.
Fonts look too small or too large UI density is not calibrated for your screen. Go to Tools > Settings and switch UI Density to Compact, Standard, or Accessible.
E-book has no cover image The file does not embed standard cover metadata. Use Tools > "Search for missing covers" or drop a JPG/PNG onto the book card.
Sync on startup takes longer than expected Slow connection or large cover downloads in progress. Startup sync runs in the background; you can continue using the application without waiting.
Forgot local app password Zero data is sent to external servers for privacy. Delete the local users.json file in the app data directory to reset local access without losing books.
