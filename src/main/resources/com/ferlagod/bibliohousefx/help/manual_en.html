1. Quick Introduction

What the app does: BiblioHouse is your ultimate local library manager. Organize physical and digital books, manage who you lend them to, and track missing volumes in your favorite sagas, all from a modern interface without uploading your data to the cloud.

Who it's for: Designed from scratch for novice readers and advanced librarians alike. No prior technical knowledge required.

Minimum requirements: Modern Windows, macOS, or Linux. Java 21+ installed. 500 MB of disk space. Screen resolution 1024x768.

Languages (100% Supported): Spanish (es), English (en), Catalan (ca), Portuguese (pt), Galician (gl), Basque (eu). You can change it from Settings > General at any time.

2. First Steps (Registration & Onboarding)

Estimated time: 2 minutes

Splash Screen and Loading

By double-clicking the BiblioHouse icon, you will see a loading window (Splash Screen) with the application's logo. This indicates that the database is initializing.

Registration and Login

The security of your collection is a priority. On first use, you must create a local user account:

  • On the Login screen, click the bottom link "Create a new account".
  • Enter your Username, Password, and repeat the password. Click Register.
  • The application will return you to the login screen. Enter your newly created credentials and click Next.

Welcome Assistant (Onboarding)

The first time you enter, the "Welcome" assistant will give you a quick tour in 3 slides. Click the blue "Next" button until you reach "Let's Start!".

3. Navigation and Structure

The layout is divided into three fundamental areas:

  • Left Sidebar (Navigation): Contains all main modules (Home, My Books, Sagas, Wishlist, History, Loans, Statistics, Settings). To go from one place to another, simply click the corresponding icon.
  • Central Panel (Workspace): Where you interact with data (lists, forms, charts).
  • Top Bar (Tools): Depending on the module, you'll find the "Add", "Edit", "Delete" buttons and the "Search" bar here.

4. Main Features (100% Screens)

Comprehensive explanation of all screens, buttons, and their effects.

Module 1: Home (Dashboard)

Purpose: Provide a quick visual summary.

Controls: There are no interactive buttons here; just cards summarizing how many books you have and what you've read this year.

Module 2: My Books (Main Inventory)

Purpose: Manage the complete CRUD (Create, Read, Update, Delete) of your collection.

Flow to add a new book:

  • Click the top left button "+ New Book".
  • The Edit Screen opens. In the "ISBN" box, type the barcode number (e.g. 9788445000663).
  • Click the Magnifying Glass next to the ISBN. The External Search Results screen will appear.
  • The app will search OpenLibrary, Google Books, and Inventaire. Click the result you prefer and hit "Import".
  • The data and cover will auto-fill. Finally, click the bottom blue Save button.

Module 3: Reading Card and Metadata Editing

When you double-click a book in "My Books", you access the Detail Card. It's a "Read-Only" mode to read synopses without accidentally deleting data.

To change data (E.g. "Reading status" to "Read"), you must right-click the book in the table and choose "Edit" (or press the pencil button at the top). On the Edit Screen you can change the title, author, rate it by stars (1 to 5), and add reviews.

Module 4: Saga Manager

Purpose: Group books that belong to the same collection.

  • Navigate to "Sagas" in the left menu.
  • Click the "+ New Saga" button. Give it a name (e.g. "Dune").
  • Select books from the right panel to add them to the saga. If you are missing Volume 2, the app will show "Missing Volume 2" in vibrant red.

Module 5: Member and Loan Management

Purpose: Know who you have lent your books to and generate their cards.

  • Navigate to "Members" or "Loans".
  • Click Add Member. Enter your friend's name (E.g. Carlos).
  • To register a loan: Go to "My Books", right-click on a book -> "Lend to...", choose Carlos.
  • Export PDF: From "Members", click "Generate PDF Card" to create a printable document with a QR code.

Module 6: Mass Scanner and Assigning Bookshelves

Scanner (Webcam): Click the top menu `Tools` > `Camera Scanner`. Show the physical book barcode to your webcam. The app will emit a green beep and instantly save it to your library.

Physical Bookshelves: Right-click on books > `Location`. Here you indicate "Living Room, Shelf 3". This helps you find the real book in your house.

Duplicates: `Tools` > `Find Duplicates`. The app tracks identical ISBNs and allows you to merge them by clicking "Merge".

5. Configuration and Languages

Access via the gear icon at the bottom left. It's divided into tabs:

  • General: Change the language between es/en/ca/pt/gl/eu. The change is applied instantly.
  • Appearance (Dark Contrast): You can select "Automatic", "Light", or "Dark".
    Accessibility Technical Note: By forcing "Dark" mode, the graphic engine replaces the CSS color variables. If you suffer from astigmatism, BiblioHouse's Dark mode uses a `#212529` (deep gray) background instead of pure black (`#000000`), avoiding the "halo" effect when reading white text.
  • Advanced: Full database export.

6. Data Management

Search is performed in the top bar of "My Books". Type "Tolkien" and the table will filter automatically. Click on the headers (e.g. "Year") to sort ascending or descending.

To export, go to `File` > `Export` and select CSV format for Excel, or JSON to perform a security dump. Importing works the same way (`File` > `Import CSV`).

7. Troubleshooting

This table covers 90% of real incidents reported by users.

SYMPTOM / PROBLEM PROBABLE CAUSE STEP-BY-STEP SOLUTION
I forgot my local app password. Being local and private, there is no recovery server. Delete the hidden users.json file in the BiblioHouse installation folder. You'll have to register a new user but you won't lose your books.
ISBN search gets stuck loading (Infinite wheel) OpenLibrary servers collapsed or no internet. Cancel the action by pressing the X. Check your WiFi. Alternatively, type the book title manually and ignore the automatic magnifying glass.
The camera scanner shows a black screen Windows/Mac is blocking the app's access to the camera for privacy. Go to your Operating System settings -> Privacy -> Camera, and allow access to Java/BiblioHouse.
"Error exporting PDF" in Member cards The destination folder (Documents) requires administrator permissions. Run BiblioHouse as Administrator or export the PDF to the "Desktop" instead.
Illegible Dark Mode (black text on dark background) The operating system is forcing a "High Contrast" theme over the application. Go to BiblioHouse Settings > Appearance. Change from "Automatic" to "Light" temporarily. Restart the app and try native "Dark".
Some buttons appear cut off in Basque/Galician Texts longer than the original FXML design box. Drag the edge of the BiblioHouse window to maximize it, the application is fully "Responsive" (adapts to width).
Missing Covers (Only a generic gray book appears) Internet failed during background image download. Go to the top Tools menu -> Select "Find missing covers". The system will scan all gray gaps and try to download them again.

8. Advanced Features (For Power-Users)

  • Quick Shortcuts: Use Ctrl+N (Add Book), Ctrl+F (Search), Del (Delete selected book).
  • Visual Drag & Drop: While on the Edit tab, you can look for a photo in your Google Chrome web browser, click, drag it, and physically drop it onto the cover slot in BiblioHouse.
  • Burst Mode (Barcode): If you use a USB "gun" laser scanner, place the cursor in the quick search. Read a book; when it beeps, press Enter. You can catalog 30 books per minute.

9. Glossary

  • Onboarding: Step-by-step assistance process for new users.
  • CRUD: Basic operations: Create, Read, Update, and Delete.
  • ISBN: International Standard Book Number of 13 digits above the barcode.
  • OpenLibrary / Inventaire: Global public databases that the app uses to autofill metadata for free.
