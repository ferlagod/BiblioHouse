1. Quick Introduction

What the app does: BiblioHouse is your ultimate personal library manager. Organize physical and digital books, read your e-books natively, manage who you lend them to (with overdue alerts), track which volumes you're missing from your favourite sagas, keep your own reading diary, locate the exact shelf of each book and sync everything with your NextCloud server, without relying on third-party cloud services.

Who it's for: Designed from scratch for novice readers and advanced librarians alike. No prior technical knowledge required.

Minimum requirements: Modern Windows, macOS or Linux. Java 21+ installed. 500 MB of disk space. Screen resolution 1024×768.

Languages (100% Supported): Spanish (es), English (en), Catalan (ca), Portuguese (pt), Galician (gl), Basque (eu). You can change the language from the Tools > Language menu or from Tools > Settings > General tab.

2. Getting Started (Welcome and Access)

Estimated time: 2 minutes

Splash Screen and Loading

When you double-click the BiblioHouse icon, you will see a loading window (Splash Screen) with the application logo. This indicates that the database is initializing.

Welcome Screen

After loading, you will see the Welcome screen with two options:

  • Start with Registration: Takes you to the Login/Registration screen to create a local user account protected with a password. Recommended for protecting your collection.
  • Start without Registration (Guest Mode): Enter directly without a password. Data is saved on your computer but will be accessible to anyone using the device. Ideal for quickly trying the app.

Registration and Login

If you choose "Start with Registration", you will reach the Login screen:

  • On the Login screen, click the bottom link "Create account".
  • Enter your Username, Password and confirm it. Click Register.
  • The application will return you to the login screen. Enter your newly created credentials and press Next.

The next time you open the app, you will be prompted for your username and password directly.

3. Navigation and Layout

The main window is divided into these areas:

  • Menu Bar (Top): Contains the menus File, Tools and Help. From here you access import/export, duplicates, settings, language, statistics and this manual.
  • Left Side Panel — "My Shelves": Displays thematic categories (Novel, Science Fiction, Fantasy, etc.) and virtual categories ("E-books", "Wishlist"). Selecting a shelf filters the main view to show only books in that category.
    Reading Challenge Widget: At the bottom of the side panel there is a widget with a progress bar showing your progress in the Annual Reading Challenge (see section Advanced Features).
  • Central Panel — Tabs: The workspace with the following tabs:
    • My Books: Visual gallery with covers from your collection.
    • Add / Manage: Search for books on OpenLibrary, scan by camera and add manually.
    • Active Loans: Register and track loans to members.
    • History: Record of all returned loans.
    • Wishlist: Books you want to get.
    • Sagas: Group books by collections or sagas.
  • Status Bar (Bottom): Shows system messages such as "Welcome, John" or the number of filtered books.

4. Main Features

Comprehensive explanation of all tabs and their functions.

Tab 1: My Books (Cover Gallery)

Purpose: View your complete collection in an attractive way.

How it works: Displays all your book covers in a gallery-style panel. Use the top-right search bar to filter by title or author. Selecting a shelf from the left panel automatically filters the gallery.

Context menu: Right-click any book to access the options:

  • 📖 Read E-book: Opens the EPUB file with BiblioHouse's native built-in reader (only for digital books).
  • Lend this book: Starts a quick loan.
  • Edit details: Opens the full editing screen.
  • Change cover: Select a local image as the cover.
  • Mark as…: Change the reading status (📋 Pending, 📖 Reading, ✅ Read).
  • 🖨️ Generate QR Label (PDF): Creates a PDF with a QR code linking to your book's details (only for physical books).
  • Delete book: Remove the book from your collection.

Double-click: Double-clicking a book opens the Detail Card in read-only mode. There you will see the cover, basic data and the new Reading Diary.

Import E-books (Drag & Drop): Drag an EPUB, PDF or MOBI file directly onto the "My Books" panel. BiblioHouse will automatically extract the metadata (title, author, cover) and add it to your collection. The panel will highlight with a blue dotted border to indicate that you can drop the file.

Tab 2: Add / Manage

Purpose: Add new books to your library in three different ways.

Method 1: OpenLibrary Search

  • In the top search bar, type the book's title or ISBN (e.g. 9788445000663).
  • Press the 🔍 button or hit Enter.
  • The Search Results window will appear with data from OpenLibrary, Google Books and Inventaire.
  • Click on the result you prefer and press "Import to Library" or "Add to Wishlist".

Method 2: Webcam Scanner

Press the 📷 button (next to the search bar). Show the book's barcode to your webcam. The app will detect it, emit a beep and search for it automatically.

Method 3: Add Manually

In the lower form "Add manually", fill in the fields (Title, Author, etc.), choose the cover and the exact locations. Then press "Add to Library".

Book Editing Screen

When you press "Edit details", a window opens with all the book's fields:

  • General Data: Title, Author, Publisher, Genre, Year, ISBN, Series/Saga and Order.
  • Physical location: You can now specify the exact shelf and rack where you keep the book at home.
  • Logical Shelves: Add or create thematic shelves (Fantasy, Novel…) and assign them.
  • Digital / Formats: Select whether you have the book as an EPUB or MOBI file and integrate the converter using Calibre.

Press Save Changes to confirm.

Tab 3: Active Loans (Smart Loans)

Purpose: Record who you lend your books to and track returns.

BiblioHouse has Smart Alerts: If you start the app or go to the main tab and have loans that have exceeded their deadline (by default, more than 30 days), the app will display an immediate warning banner (e.g. "You have 2 overdue books pending return").

  • Select a Book and a Member from the dropdown.
  • Press "Make Loan".
  • To register a return, select the loan and press "Mark as Returned". The loan will move to the History tab.

Tab 4: History

Purpose: View the historical record of all completed loans, with their respective start and return dates.

Tab 5: Wishlist

Purpose: Save books you want to get without mixing them with your main collection. When you acquire them, right-click and select "Mark as acquired".

Tab 6: Sagas and Collections

Purpose: Group books that belong to the same series or collection.

The tab has a split panel: "My Collections" on the left, and the saga's volumes on the right.

Context menu: When clicking on a specific book within a saga, you can now select "Unlink book from saga" to remove it from the collection without deleting the book from your main database.

Missing Volumes Warning: If BiblioHouse detects that you have Volume 1 and Volume 3 of a saga, it will display a temporary "Volume 2 missing" card so you know your collection is incomplete.

Member Management

Access from the Active Loans tab > "Manage Members" button. You can create, edit and generate a PDF with the membership cards for each registered member.

Statistics

Access from the Tools > Statistics menu. Enjoy an advanced graphical analysis with your annual progress, genre pie chart and top 10 authors.

5. Built-in E-book Reader

BiblioHouse now includes a completely native EPUB file reader, designed so that you don't need to leave the app to read.

  • To open it, right-click on a digital book in "My Books" and select "📖 Read E-book".
  • Progress synchronization: The application will automatically save the reading percentage where you left off, so that the next time you open it or sync with Nextcloud, you'll continue on exactly the same page.
  • If the book was set to "Pending", the app will automatically change its status to "Reading".
  • It features a clean design, similar to commercial e-readers, optimizing margins and colours for reading.

6. Reading Diary and Exact Locations

BiblioHouse transforms the cataloguing experience into a true personal reading log:

  • Reading Diary: When double-clicking a book (Detail Card), you will find a section in the side panel to add Notes and literal Quotes (with their respective page number). Perfect for jotting down reflections while you read or highlighting memorable passages.
  • Advanced Physical Management: Do you have boxes in the attic and can't find your copy of *The Lord of the Rings*? Use the "Location" field (e.g. "Living Room Shelf, Rack 3").
  • QR Code Labels: Right-click the book > "Generate QR Label (PDF)". Print these labels to stick on the spine or inside your physical books and scan the code with your phone to view all the details of the work.

7. Settings

Access via the Tools > Settings menu. It is divided into four tabs:

General Tab

  • User Preferences: Ask for confirmation before deleting, Loan day limit (default 30), etc.
  • Appearance and Languages:
    • Language: Spanish, English, Catalan, Galician, Basque and Portuguese. Translations have been integrated throughout the entire interface without needing to restart the app.
    • Visual Theme:
      • Light/Dark (Primer): Classic GitHub-style theme.
      • Nord Light/Dark: Cold, arctic tones that reduce visual fatigue.
      • Cupertino Light/Dark: Fluid macOS-style aesthetics.
      • Dracula: Vibrant dark theme with cyan and pink accents, loved by developers.

Data Tab

Here you can view the local location of the files and create a backup in ZIP format. In the Danger Zone you can clear your database or perform a full wipe (Factory Reset).

Synchronization Tab (NextCloud)

Configure your private NextCloud server. This option also automatically syncs your covers and notes in the background every time you make a change.

8. Data Management (Import, Export and Backup)

All import and export options are in the File menu:

  • Import database: Load a previously exported BiblioHouse JSON file.
  • Import from CSV: Bulk import books using CSV templates.
  • Export PDF Report: Generate professional reports applying multiple cross-filters (Genres, years, shelves).
  • Export Web Catalogue (HTML): Generate a web page with your entire collection and embedded covers (ideal for sharing with friends via WhatsApp or Telegram).

9. NextCloud Synchronization

Purpose: Keep a synchronized copy of your library (with your e-books and covers) on your own NextCloud server via WebDAV.

Access from Tools > Settings > Synchronization tab.

  • Enter the Server URL (e.g. https://your-nextcloud.example.com).
  • Enter your NextCloud Username and your Application password (generated in Nextcloud's security settings; never enter your main Nextcloud password here).
  • Press "Test connection" and then use the manual options to upload or download data if it's the first time.

10. Troubleshooting

This table covers 90% of real incidents reported by users.

SYMPTOM / PROBLEM PROBABLE CAUSE STEP-BY-STEP SOLUTION
I forgot my local app password. Being local and private, there is no recovery server. Delete the hidden file users.json in the BiblioHouse installation folder to force a new registration (your books will not be lost).
ISBN search keeps loading OpenLibrary servers overloaded. Cancel the search and add the book manually.
EPUB conversion error The program cannot find the Calibre executable. Install Calibre on your PC and make sure it is included in your system's PATH variables.
"Error exporting PDF" The destination folder requires administrator permissions. Save the report or card directly to the "Desktop" instead.
Missing Covers Internet failed during download. Go to the Tools > "Search for missing covers" menu.

11. Advanced Features (For Power Users)

  • Keyboard Shortcuts:
    • Ctrl+N / ⌘+N — Go to "Add/Manage".
    • Ctrl+F / ⌘+F — Search on OpenLibrary.
    • Ctrl+L / ⌘+L — Active Loans tab.
    • F1 — Open this User Manual.
  • Drag & Drop (Covers): Drag images from the internet directly onto your book's thumbnail.
  • Burst Mode (Barcode): With a USB laser scanner focus on the ISBN field. You can catalogue dozens of books per minute.
  • Annual Reading Challenge: In the side panel, you can set how many books you want to read this year; the app recalculates your progress with every book you mark as "Read".

12. Glossary

  • ISBN: International Standard Book Number, a 13-digit code.
  • OpenLibrary: Public database used to import metadata for free.
  • NextCloud: Private cloud to securely sync your BiblioHouse data.
  • EPUB: Universal open reading format for electronic books.
  • Saga/Collection: Grouping of books belonging to the same literary series.
