1. Quick Introduction

What the App Does: BiblioHouse is your ultimate personal library manager. Organize physical and digital books, manage loans to friends, track missing volumes in your favorite sagas, maintain a wishlist, and sync everything with your NextCloud server—without relying on third-party cloud services.

Who It's For: Designed from scratch for both novice readers and advanced librarians. No prior technical knowledge required.

Minimum Requirements: Windows, macOS, or modern Linux. Java 21+ installed. 500 MB disk space. Screen resolution 1024x768.

Fully Supported Languages: Spanish (es), English (en), Catalan (ca), Portuguese (pt), Galician (gl), Basque (eu). You can change the language from the Tools > Language menu or via Tools > Settings > General tab.

2. Getting Started (Welcome and Access)

Estimated time: 2 minutes

Splash Screen and Loading

When you double-click the BiblioHouse icon, you'll see a loading screen (Splash Screen) with the app logo. This indicates that the database is initializing.

Welcome Screen

After loading, you'll see the Welcome screen with two options:

  • Start with Registration: Takes you to the Login/Registration screen to create a local user account protected with a password. Recommended to protect your collection.
  • Start without Registration (Guest Mode): Enters directly without a password. Data is saved on your computer but will be accessible to anyone using the device. Ideal for quickly testing the app.

Registration and Login

If you choose "Start with Registration," you'll reach the Login screen:

  • On the Login screen, click the "Create Account" link at the bottom.
  • Enter your Username, Password, and confirm it. Click Register.
  • The app will take you back to the login screen. Enter your newly created credentials and click Next.

Next time you open the app, you'll be prompted for your username and password directly.

3. Navigation and Structure

The main window is divided into these areas:

  • Menu Bar (Top): Contains the File, Tools, and Help menus. From here, you can import/export, find duplicates, access settings, change language, view statistics, and open this manual.
  • Left Sidebar — "My Shelves": Displays thematic categories (Novel, Science Fiction, Fantasy, etc.). Selecting a shelf filters the main view to show only books from that category.
  • Central Panel — Tabs: The workspace with the following tabs:
    • My Books: Visual gallery with book covers from your collection.
    • Add / Manage: Search for books on OpenLibrary, scan via webcam, and add manually.
    • Active Loans: Register and manage loans to members.
    • History: Record of all returned loans.
    • Wishlist: Books you want to acquire.
    • Sagas: Group books by collections or sagas.
  • Status Bar (Bottom): Displays system messages like "Welcome, John" or the number of filtered books.

4. Main Features

Comprehensive explanation of all tabs and their functions.

Tab 1: My Books (Cover Gallery)

Purpose: Visually display your entire collection attractively.

How It Works: Shows all your book covers in a gallery-style panel. Use the search bar in the top-right to filter by title or author. Selecting a shelf from the left panel automatically filters the gallery.

Context Menu: Right-click any book to access options:

  • Lend This Book: Start a quick loan.
  • Edit Details: Open the full edit screen.
  • Change Cover: Select a local image as the cover.
  • Mark as... Change reading status (📋 Pending, 📖 Reading, ✅ Read).
  • Delete Book: Remove the book from your collection.

Double Click: Double-clicking a book opens its Detail Card in read-only mode, showing the cover, title, author, publisher, year, genre, ISBN, star rating, and personal review. From there, you can click "Edit Details" to modify the book.

Tab 2: Add / Manage

Purpose: Add new books to your library in three different ways.

Method 1: Search on OpenLibrary

  • In the top search bar, enter the book's title or ISBN (e.g., 9788445000663).
  • Click the 🔍 button or press Enter.
  • The Search Results window will appear with data from OpenLibrary, Google Books, and Inventaire.
  • Click your preferred result and press "Import to Library" or "Add to Wishlist".

Method 2: Webcam Barcode Scanner

Click the 📷 button (next to the search bar). Show the book's barcode to your webcam. The app will detect it, beep, and automatically search for it.

Method 3: Add Manually

In the "Add Manually" form at the bottom, fill in the fields:

  • Title, Author, Publisher, Genre, ISBN, Year, Quantity.
  • Series/Saga and Order (for grouping in collections).
  • Select a cover image using the "Select..." button.
  • Check the "I already own it" box if you possess the book.
  • Click "Add to Library".

Book Edit Screen

Clicking "Edit Details" (from the context menu or detail card) opens a window with all the book's fields:

  • Cover: Change the image with "Change Cover" (or drag an image from your browser) and "Search Online" to automatically download a cover.
  • Reading Status: Select "Pending," "Reading," or "Read" from the dropdown.
  • Rating: Rate it from 0 to 5 stars.
  • Data: Title, Author, Publisher, Genre, Year, ISBN, Series/Saga, and Order.
  • Shelves: Add or create custom shelves and assign them to the book.
  • Personal Review: Write your impressions about the book.
  • "Owned": Uncheck this box if you don't physically have the book yet.

Click Save Changes to confirm or Cancel to discard.

Tab 3: Active Loans

Purpose: Register to whom you lend your books and track returns.

  • Select a Book from the "Book to Lend" dropdown.
  • Select a Member from the dropdown. If they don't exist, click the "+" button to create a quick member, or "Manage Members" for full management.
  • Click "Make Loan".
  • The loan will appear in the table below with the date and estimated return date.
  • To register a return, select the loan and click "Mark as Returned". The loan will move to the History tab.

Overdue Loan Warning: When starting the app, if there are books with loans exceeding the configured day limit, you'll receive a warning with the pending returns.

Tab 4: History

Purpose: View the historical record of all completed loans.

Displays a table with columns: Book, Member, Loan Date, and Return Date.

Tab 5: Wishlist

Purpose: Save books you want to acquire without mixing them with your collection.

  • Click "Search to Add..." to search for a book on OpenLibrary.
  • In the search results, click "Add to Wishlist" instead of "Import."
  • Wishlist books appear as cards in this tab.
  • When you acquire a book, right-click and select "Mark as Acquired" to move it to your main library.

Tab 6: Sagas and Collections

Purpose: Group books that belong to the same series or collection.

The tab has a split panel:

  • Left Panel — "My Collections": List of all your sagas. Click one to view its books.
  • Right Panel: Gallery of covers for books in that saga, with an informative summary.

Context Menu in Saga List:

  • Rename Saga: Change the collection's name.
  • Delete Saga (Keep Books): Delete the grouping but keep the books.
  • Delete Saga and Its Books: Delete the saga and all its associated books.

Books are automatically assigned to sagas when you fill in the "Series/Saga" field when adding or editing a book.

Member Management

Purpose: Manage the people to whom you lend books.

Access via the Active Loans tab > "Manage Members" button.

  • View the table of all registered members (Member No., Name, Surname, ID, Address).
  • Edit Selected: Modify a member's data.
  • Print ID Cards (PDF): Generate a printable PDF document with member ID cards.
  • Delete Member: Remove a member from the database.

Statistics

Purpose: Get a visual analysis of your collection.

Access via the Tools > Statistics menu. The window shows:

  • Key Metrics: Total books, Read books (with percentage), Most frequent author, Main genre, Average rating with stars, Finished this year, and Average rate (books/month).
  • Pie Chart: Distribution by Genre.
  • Bar Chart: Top 10 Most Frequent Authors.
  • Stacked Bar Chart: Reading Status by Genre.

5. Settings

Access via the Tools > Settings menu. It is divided into four tabs:

General Tab

  • User Preferences:
    • Ask for confirmation before deleting a book.
    • Always start in full screen.
    • Loan limit days (default: 30).
  • Appearance:
    • Language: Switch between Spanish, English, Catalan, Galician, Basque, and Portuguese. The change applies instantly without restarting.
    • Visual Theme: Choose between "Light (Primer Light)" and "Dark (Primer Dark)".
      Accessibility Note: Dark mode uses a deep gray background (#212529) instead of pure black (#000000) to avoid the "halo" effect for people with astigmatism.
  • Personalization: Change your library's name (appears in the title bar).

Data Tab

  • File Location: Shows the path of the local database. You can open the folder with one click.
  • Export Backup: Generates a ZIP file with all your library data (books, members, covers, preferences).
  • Danger Zone:
    • Delete ALL Books: Empties the library (asks for confirmation).
    • Factory Reset: Deletes absolutely everything (books, members, covers, credentials, and settings). Requires typing "DELETE" as a security confirmation. The app will close and, upon reopening, will be as if freshly installed.

Synchronization Tab (NextCloud)

See section 7. NextCloud Synchronization for all details.

About Tab

Displays the logo, current BiblioHouse version, author, and a link to support the project on Liberapay.

Remember to click "Save Settings" to apply changes or "Cancel" to discard them.

6. Data Management (Import, Export, and Backup)

All import and export options are in the File menu:

  • Import Database: Load a previously exported BiblioHouse JSON file.
  • Import from CSV: Import books from a CSV file (Excel-compatible).
  • Export: Export your library in JSON format for backup.
  • Export PDF Report: Generate a professional PDF report of your library with advanced filters (by title, author, shelves, genres, year range, and reading status).
  • Export Web Catalog: Generate a navigable HTML file of your collection, ideal for sharing your catalog on the web. You can group by genre or shelf and apply filters.

Local Search: Performed in the top bar of the "My Books" tab. Type "Tolkien," and the gallery will automatically filter with a slight delay to avoid slowing down typing.

7. NextCloud Synchronization

Purpose: Keep a synchronized copy of your library on your own NextCloud server via WebDAV, without relying on third-party cloud services.

Access via Tools > Settings > Synchronization tab.

  • Enter the Server URL (e.g., https://your-nextcloud.example.com).
  • Enter your NextCloud Username.
  • Enter the Application Password (generated from Settings > Security in your NextCloud panel). This password is stored encrypted in the operating system's keychain, never in plain text.
  • Click "Test Connection" to verify the data is correct.
  • "Sync Now (Upload ↑)": Upload your local database to NextCloud.
  • "Restore from NextCloud (Download ↓)": Download and overwrite your local data with NextCloud data (asks for confirmation).

Auto-Synchronization: If NextCloud is configured, the app automatically uploads your data whenever you make significant changes (adding, editing, or deleting books).

8. Troubleshooting Common Issues

This table covers 90% of real incidents reported by users.

SYMPTOM / ISSUE LIKELY CAUSE STEP-BY-STEP SOLUTION
I forgot my local app password. Since it's local and private, there's no recovery server. Delete the hidden file users.json in the BiblioHouse installation folder. You'll need to register a new user, but you won't lose your books. Alternatively, reopen the app and choose "Start without Registration" to access in guest mode.
ISBN search keeps loading (infinite spinner) OpenLibrary servers are down or you have no internet. Cancel the action by closing the results window. Check your connection. Alternatively, manually enter the book's data in the "Add Manually" section.
Camera scanner shows a black screen The operating system blocks the app's camera access for privacy. Go to your OS Settings > Privacy > Camera, and allow access to Java/BiblioHouse.
"Error exporting PDF" for Member ID Cards The destination folder requires administrator permissions. Run BiblioHouse as Administrator or export the PDF to the "Desktop" instead.
Dark Mode is unreadable (black text on dark background) The operating system is forcing a "High Contrast" theme on the app. Go to Tools > Settings > General tab. Temporarily change the Visual Theme to "Light (Primer Light)". Restart the app and try "Dark (Primer Dark)" again.
Some buttons appear cut off in Basque/Galician Longer texts than the original design box. Drag the edge of the BiblioHouse window to maximize it. The app is fully "responsive" and adapts to width.
Missing Covers (Only a generic gray book appears) Internet failed during background image download. Go to the Tools menu > "Find Missing Covers". The system will review all books without covers and try downloading them again.
NextCloud connection error Incorrect URL, username/password, or the server is inaccessible. Verify the URL (must include https://). Ensure you're using an application password generated from NextCloud (Settings > Security), not your regular password. Click "Test Connection" for a detailed error diagnosis.
The app detects updates but doesn't install them automatically BiblioHouse only notifies about new versions; it doesn't auto-update. Visit bibliohouse.org and manually download the latest version.

9. Advanced Features (For Power Users)

  • Keyboard Shortcuts:
    • Ctrl+N / ⌘+N — Go to the "Add/Manage" tab and focus the title field to manually add a book.
    • Ctrl+F / ⌘+F — Go to the "Add/Manage" tab and focus the OpenLibrary search bar.
    • Ctrl+L / ⌘+L — Go to the Active Loans tab.
    • Ctrl+Q / ⌘+Q — Close the application.
    • F1 — Open this User Manual.
  • Drag & Drop Visual: While in the Book Edit screen, you can search for a photo in your web browser, drag it, and drop it directly onto the cover area in BiblioHouse to change it instantly.
  • Burst Mode (Barcode): If you use a USB laser scanner "gun," place the cursor in the OpenLibrary searcher. Scan a barcode; when detected, press Enter. You can catalog dozens of books per minute.
  • Find Missing Sagas: From the Tools menu > "Find Missing Sagas Online", the app searches public databases for missing volumes in your sagas and suggests which ones to get.
  • Update Check: On startup, BiblioHouse silently checks if a new version is available and notifies you with a banner in the top bar.

10. Glossary

  • CRUD: Basic operations: Create, Read, Update, Delete.
  • ISBN: 13-digit International Standard Book Number. Found above the barcode on the back cover.
  • OpenLibrary / Inventaire / Google Books: Global public databases the app uses to auto-complete book metadata for free.
  • NextCloud: Self-hosted cloud storage platform. BiblioHouse uses it to sync your library with your own server.
  • WebDAV: Remote file access protocol used by NextCloud for synchronization.
  • Guest Mode: Access the app without creating a password-protected account. Data is saved locally without protection.
  • Shelf: Thematic category (e.g., Novel, Fantasy) used to organize and filter books.
  • Saga/Collection: Grouping of books that belong to the same literary series.
