BiblioHouse v1.6 - User Manual

Welcome to BiblioHouse. This version introduces powerful automation tools and a complete redesign of the collections system.

1. Library Management

Bulk Import (CSV) Improved

Import data from Goodreads or Bookwyrm. The system now retrieves publisher information, personal reviews, ratings, and tags automatically.

2. Covers and Metadata

Bulk Cover Scanner New

Go to Tools > Find missing covers. The app will scan your entire library and download missing covers from Google Books and OpenLibrary.

3. Sagas and Collections Redesigned

Collections now have their own **dedicated tab**.

  • Gap Detection: The app highlights missing volumes in red.
  • Saga Scanner: Use Tools > Complete Sagas to automatically identify which series your books belong to.
  • Context Menu: Right-click a saga to Rename or delete it (with or without its books).
